# Personal case store

Keep material incidents and useful outcomes here for later guidance review.
These records connect closeouts, guidance updates, and later follow-ups across sessions.

- `cases/`: case records; use the example in `templates/case.md` as a starting point.
- `evidence/`: the minimum supporting material needed to assess a case.
- `resolved-cases.md`: one row per retired case, created with the first retirement.

Search for related records before writing and append follow-ups to the same incident.
Link an earlier improvement when relevant, retaining its expected effect, later evidence, and any still-open observation question.
Preserve original observations, distinguish evidence from interpretations, and omit secrets and unnecessary private details.

Retire a case once its decision is settled: an improvement addressing it was applied, no change is needed, or it duplicates another record.
Carry any open question and the evidence it needs into the record that tracks it, then delete the case and its exclusive evidence.
Record the case ID, a short title, severity, resolution, and retirement date as one row of `resolved-cases.md`.

Recording authorization comes from the user's request, applicable guidance, or personal recording configuration.
This template grants no additional permission for commits, publication, or guidance changes.
