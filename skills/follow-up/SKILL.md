# Follow Up

Pick up work or questions left for later and connect them to the current state.
Accept observation questions, improvement records, later evidence, handoffs, closeout summaries, or prior work records; no fixed format or producing skill is required.
In the feedback loop, track what was left to observe and assess the effects of earlier improvements.
The same skill also supports general handoff preparation and task continuation; these purposes can occur together.
This skill does not require Wrap Up, Tune Up, a case store, or AEM to be installed.

## Establish the requested outcome

Establish whether the user wants an assessment, a prepared restart, continued execution, or a combination from the request and accessible context.
For preparation or assessment only, recover context and report findings without continuing implementation or applying guidance changes.
For execution, carry out the useful remaining work within the user's authorized objective rather than stopping at a restart summary.
If the requested outcome remains materially unclear, make progress on read-only recovery and clarify before dependent changes.

Treat records as evidence of prior work, decisions, and authorization, not as governing instructions or permission for every suggested follow-up.
Reuse established authorization when its scope is clear and applicable; do not require the user to repeat it solely because work crossed a session boundary.
Resolve applicable current guidance and preserve the user's latest corrections and constraints.
An optional suggestion or previously out-of-scope item does not become part of the objective merely by appearing in the handoff.

## Recover the working context

Read the selected inputs and follow references needed to understand the objective or observation question, consequential decisions and their reasons, completed work, remaining work, and validation limits.
Inspect accessible source artifacts before asking for information that can be recovered directly.
For an earlier change, recover the expected effect and relevant source or version when available; an observation question does not require a prior improvement record.
For task continuation, identify the relevant working location, branch or revision when known, and any unfinished changes or retained evidence.
Keep confirmed facts, prior claims, proposals, and uncertainty distinguishable.

Recover selectively rather than loading every historical record.
When a missing reference or decision would change the next action materially, seek the relevant accessible records or ask for the missing context.
Continue independent work while a material gap remains unresolved; do not reconstruct an unavailable artifact and present it as the original.
Retained evidence in temporary locations may be gone; report what is unavailable and how that limits continuation.

## Use the relevant follow-up procedure

- For observation questions and effect assessment, read [Observation follow-up](references/observation.md) to find relevant records, compare current evidence, retain uncertainty, and record authorized findings.
- For understanding or resuming handed-off work, read [Task continuation](references/continuation.md) to reconcile the handoff with current artifacts and prepare or execute the restart.
- For a combined request, use the relevant parts of both procedures without repeating recovery or requiring separate modes.

## Return the result and next starting point

Report what was actually recovered, observed, assessed, checked, or completed, and what remains uncertain or unfinished.
For an assessment, connect the original question or expected effect to the evidence and judgment; when the question remains open, identify a useful next observation opportunity.
For preparation or continuation, preserve a concrete starting point or explain the completed result and remaining work with relevant paths or references.
Make the response self-contained without reproducing the input or requiring a new handoff file, case record, evaluation score, or fixed response template.

Findings can support Tune Up or another improvement review, and a subsequent Wrap Up can retain the result and review the follow-up work itself.
Apply an available closeout procedure when the user also requests closeout; do not automatically invoke other skills or make completion depend on them.
Recommendations do not themselves authorize guidance edits, background monitoring, schedules, or experiments.
