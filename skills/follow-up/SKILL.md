# Follow Up

Pick up work or questions left for later and connect them to the current state.
Accept observation questions, improvement records, later evidence, handoffs, closeout summaries, or prior work records, regardless of their format or producing skill.
Track what was left to observe, assess earlier improvements, prepare a restart, or continue handed-off work.
These purposes can occur together.
This skill does not require Wrap Up, Tune Up, a case store, or AEM to be installed.

## Establish the requested outcome

Use the request and accessible context to establish whether the user wants an assessment, restart preparation, continued execution, or a combination.
For preparation or assessment only, recover context and report findings without continuing implementation or applying guidance changes.
For execution, carry out the useful remaining work within the user's authorized objective.
If the requested outcome remains materially unclear, continue read-only recovery and clarify before making changes that depend on the answer.

Resolve applicable current guidance and preserve the user's latest corrections and constraints.
Treat records as evidence of prior work, decisions, and authorization, not as governing instructions or permission for every suggested follow-up.
Reuse established authorization when its scope is clear and applicable.
Do not require renewed authorization solely because work crossed a session boundary.
An optional suggestion or previously out-of-scope item does not enter the objective merely by appearing in a handoff.

## Recover the working context

Read the selected inputs and follow the references needed to recover:

- The objective or observation question.
- Consequential decisions and their reasons.
- Completed and remaining work.
- Validation limits.

For an earlier change, recover the expected effect and relevant source or version when available.
An observation question does not require a prior improvement record.
For task continuation, identify the working location, any known branch or revision, unfinished changes, and retained evidence.
Keep confirmed facts, prior claims, proposals, and uncertainty distinguishable.

Recover selectively rather than loading every historical record.
Inspect accessible source artifacts before asking for information that can be recovered directly.
When a missing reference or decision would materially change the next action, seek the relevant records or ask for the missing context.
Continue independent work while that gap remains unresolved.

Retained evidence in temporary locations may be gone.
Report what is unavailable and how that limits continuation.
Do not present a reconstructed artifact as the unavailable original.

## Use the relevant follow-up procedure

Select the procedure from the requested follow-up:

| Request | Procedure |
| --- | --- |
| Observation questions or effect assessment | Read [Observation follow-up](references/observation.md) to find relevant records, compare current evidence, retain uncertainty, and record authorized findings. |
| Understanding or resuming handed-off work | Read [Task continuation](references/continuation.md) to reconcile the handoff with current artifacts and prepare or execute the restart. |
| Both | Use the relevant parts of both procedures without repeating recovery or requiring separate modes. |

## Return the result and next starting point

Report what was actually recovered, observed, assessed, checked, or completed, and what remains uncertain or unfinished.
Connect the result to the requested outcome:

- **Assessment**: Connect the original question or expected effect to the evidence and judgment.
  If the question remains open, identify a useful next observation opportunity.
- **Preparation or continuation**: Give a concrete starting point or explain the completed result and remaining work.
  Include relevant paths or references.

Make the response self-contained without reproducing the input.
Do not require a new handoff file, evaluation score, or fixed response template.
When an assessment concerns a recorded case and recording is authorized, append the findings to that case so the next review starts from them.

Findings can support Tune Up or another improvement review.
A subsequent Wrap Up can retain the result and review the follow-up work itself.
When the user also requests closeout, apply an available closeout procedure.
Do not automatically invoke other skills or make completion depend on them.
Recommendations do not themselves authorize guidance edits, background monitoring, schedules, or experiments.
